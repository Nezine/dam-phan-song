# Sources — skill-content.json

Dataset for the in-app AI (5 skill rooms, Vietnamese). Every technique, quiz
question and exercise traces to one of the sources below. All sources are
free and public. No paywalled books, no pirated content, no invented
statistics. **All sources accessed 2026-11-14.**

## Room 1 — Thuyết phục mua hàng (sales-persuasion)

- https://hbr.org/2013/07/the-uses-and-abuses-of-influence — Cialdini's 6 principles of influence (HBR)
- https://www.retorio.com/blog/what-spin-selling — SPIN selling, 4 question types (Retorio)
- https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/handling-common-sales-objections — LAER 5-step objection handling + BANT objection groups (HubSpot; URL cleaned of tracking parameters)
- https://www.pipedrive.com/en/blog/how-to-close-a-sale — closing techniques + active listening (Pipedrive)

  it is traced to the scaffolding source (alternative explanations / real-life
  metaphors) and Feynman (analogies), stated at general-principle level only.
- Room 4 (public speaking): no dedicated source on handling audience Q&A was
  opened in the time available; Q&A is only lightly folded into the MindTools
  "pause to collect your thoughts" technique.
- Room 5 (wealth mindset): time management / prioritization has no dedicated
  source; it is folded into the "systems over goals" technique (James Clear).
  No Eisenhower matrix or Pomodoro content was added (no source opened).
- Room 2 (income negotiation): no Glassdoor / Indeed source was opened directly
  (only seen in search snippets), so none is listed; no Vietnam-specific salary
  market source was used. No salary statistics are stated anywhere in the file.
- Room 1 (sales persuasion): the SPIN source mentions Rackham's 35,000-call
  study; that number was deliberately NOT included in the dataset.
- Room 5: no absolute statistics are stated; the "37x better in a year" line
  from James Clear is not presented as a statistic in the file.
